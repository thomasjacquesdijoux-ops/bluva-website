Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. About BLUVA

BLUVA is a software solution currently under development, designed to help accounting firms and businesses automate the reconciliation and accounting preparation of online payment transactions.

BLUVA is developed and operated from Belgium.

2. Information We May Process

When a merchant connects a payment service provider such as PayPal to BLUVA, and explicitly authorizes access, BLUVA may process transaction-related information necessary for accounting reconciliation.

This may include:

  • Transaction identifiers
  • Transaction dates
  • Transaction amounts and currencies
  • Payment fees
  • Refunds
  • Chargebacks and disputes
  • Payouts and transfers
  • Currency conversion information
  • Payment references
  • Invoice references
  • Customer or payer information when included in the transaction data

3. Purpose of Processing

Transaction data is processed exclusively for purposes related to:

  • Accounting reconciliation
  • Matching payments with invoices
  • Classifying payment transactions
  • Preparing accounting data
  • Reducing manual accounting work
  • Generating structured accounting exports

BLUVA does not use payment transaction data for advertising, profiling, or selling personal information.

4. PayPal Data

BLUVA intends to access PayPal transaction information only after explicit authorization from the relevant PayPal merchant.

PayPal data will be used in read-only mode for financial reporting, reconciliation and accounting automation.

BLUVA does not initiate PayPal payments, hold funds, manage merchant balances, or transfer funds on behalf of users.

5. Data Sharing

BLUVA does not sell personal or transaction data.

Data may only be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the platform, provide the requested accounting integration, comply with legal obligations, or at the explicit request of the customer.

6. Data Security

BLUVA is designed to apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect transaction and accounting data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

API credentials and authentication tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not embedded directly in application source code.

7. Data Retention

Data will only be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested reconciliation and accounting services, comply with applicable legal requirements, and maintain appropriate accounting and audit records.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, users may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of their personal data.

Users may also revoke access granted to connected third-party services such as PayPal.

9. Cookies

The current BLUVA website does not use advertising cookies or behavioral tracking technologies.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as BLUVA develops and additional integrations or services are introduced.
