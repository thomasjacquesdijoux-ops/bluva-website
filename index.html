About BLUVA

BLUVA is a SaaS solution designed to automate the accounting preparation and reconciliation of online payment transactions.

The platform connects payment service providers with invoice and accounting data in order to reduce manual reconciliation work.

How it works

BLUVA retrieves payment transaction data, classifies payments, fees, refunds, chargebacks and payouts, and matches customer payments with invoice information.

The resulting enriched accounting data can then be transmitted or imported into accounting software.

PayPal integration

PayPal integration is currently in development.

BLUVA intends to use PayPal transaction reporting APIs in read-only mode, with the merchant's explicit authorization, for accounting reconciliation purposes.

BLUVA does not initiate payments, hold funds or manage merchant balances.

For accounting firms

BLUVA helps accounting firms automate the processing of payment data from payment service providers and reduce manual accounting operations.
